HOW TO PLAY:

  1. Pick your opponent, then hit START.
  2. Put your dice in order, lowest to highest, before your opponent finishes.
  3. Drag a die with your mouse or finger. Or focus a die and move it with the left and right arrow keys, with Home and End sending it to either end.
  4. Prefer not to drag? Press Enter on a die to pick it up, then Enter on another to place it there. Escape puts it back.
  5. Hit DONE to lock in your order. Switch between 6 and 9 dice any time.
