How to play: put your dice in ascending order before your opponent finishes. Drag a die, or focus one and move it with the left and right arrow keys, with Home and End sending it to either end. Press Enter or Space on a die to pick it up, then Enter or Space on another die to place it there. Escape cancels. Press DONE to submit your order.

HOW TO PLAY
  1. Pick your opponent, then hit START.
  2. Sort your dice from lowest to highest before your opponent finishes.
  3. Drag to reorder. Keyboard: Left/Right, Home/End, or Enter to pick up and place a die. Escape cancels.
  4. Hit DONE to submit your order. Switch between 6 and 9 dice anytime.
